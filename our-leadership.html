School Leadership

Message from the Secretary

✦ Secretary ✦
"A school is not merely a building — it is a place where futures are forged, character is strengthened, and leaders are born."

As Secretary of Dnyanda English Medium School, I consider it my highest privilege to lead and serve an institution dedicated to shaping tomorrow's leaders. My commitment has always been to build a school where academic excellence walks hand in hand with strong moral values, discipline, and a sense of social responsibility.

I firmly believe that every child carries within him or her the potential for greatness. It is our duty as educators and administrators to nurture that potential — to provide the right environment, the right guidance, and the right opportunities so that each student can rise to his or her fullest capability.

I extend my heartfelt welcome to every parent and student who chooses to be a part of the Dnyanda family. Together, let us build a legacy of knowledge, integrity, and achievement that our children will carry forward with pride.

Sudhir V. Mungase
Secretary
Dnyanda English Medium School
From the Principal's Desk

A Message from the Principal

"Education should shape both the mind and the heart."

At Dnyanda English Medium School, we strive to nurture children who are knowledgeable, disciplined, confident, and compassionate. Along with academic excellence, we emphasize values such as honesty, respect, responsibility, kindness, and lifelong learning.

Our dedicated team works closely with parents to create a positive environment where every child feels encouraged to grow, explore new ideas, and achieve their full potential.

Together, we are building a generation that is prepared not only for examinations but also for life — ready to face the world with courage, compassion, and confidence.

Mrs.Velegrin Paul - Principal
Dnyanda English Medium School

What We Stand For

Our Leadership Values

The principles that guide every decision, every interaction, and every lesson at Dnyanda.

Vision

We look beyond the present to build a future-ready institution where every student's potential is fully realised.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct form the bedrock of our leadership and institutional culture.

Innovation

We continuously explore new teaching methods, technologies, and approaches to make learning engaging and effective.

Student First

Every policy, program, and decision is made with the student's best interest, well-being, and growth at heart.

Academic Excellence

We set and maintain the highest academic standards while ensuring learning remains joyful, inclusive, and meaningful.

Community

We believe school is a community — students, parents, staff, and society working together toward shared success.
