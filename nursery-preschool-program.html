Nursery / Preschool Program

A joyful early learning program designed to build confidence, curiosity, language skills, social habits, and foundational readiness through play-based activities.

A Joyful Start to Learning

Our Nursery / Preschool Program creates a warm, safe, and engaging environment where young children learn through play, exploration, stories, music, movement, art, and hands-on activities. The program focuses on developing early language, sensory awareness, social confidence, emotional expression, and basic learning readiness.

Key Focus Areas

  • Sensory training
  • Language development
  • Fine and gross motor skills
  • Social habits and sharing
  • Creative expression
  • Storytelling and rhymes
  • Play-based learning
  • Early numeracy & recognition

Our Learning Approach

Children learn best when they feel happy, safe, and involved. Our preschool approach uses activity-based and experiential methods to help children observe, ask questions, interact, express themselves, and develop confidence at their own pace.

Why Choose Our Preschool Program?

  • Safe & Caring Classrooms: Designed with child-friendly furniture and safety features to give parents complete peace of mind.
  • Joyful Teaching Methods: Lessons are structured around activities, art, rhymes, and stories rather than formal study pressure.
  • Value-Based Focus: Helping children build good manners, basic sharing habits, and respect for elders early on.
  • Experiential Learning: Sensory activities and hands-on toys stimulate logical and cognitive development.
  • Primary School Readiness: Smoothly bridging the transition from home to formal elementary primary grade classrooms.

Program Details

  • Age Group 3 - 5 Years
  • Section Level Foundational Stage
  • KG Grades Nursery, LKG, UKG
