Science & Technology Model Exhibition

A creative exhibition where students present science models, technology ideas, experiments, and innovative learning projects.

About the Event

The Science & Technology Model Exhibition is a celebration of student creativity, curiosity, and practical learning. The school auditorium is transformed into a vibrant innovation lab where students present working projects, charts, and robotic modules that they have conceptualized and built during school hours.

Key Event Highlights

  • Student-built physics & chemistry models
  • Robotics design and obstacle runs
  • Interactive math and logic games
  • Public presentation by students
  • Evaluation by guest science experts
  • Young Inventor award certificates

What Students Will Experience

Students get to step into the shoes of young scientists. They set up their exhibition counters, explain the logic behind their working science models, answer questions from visiting parents, and perform live technology experiments. This hands-on process boosts confidence, public speaking habits, and social collaboration.

Why This Event Matters

Rote memorization doesn't build innovators. Experiential projects help clarify complex theories of electricity, pressure, force, and programming. By working on tangible models, students build critical thinking skills, analytical resilience, and a life-long love for science.
