Student Achievements & Alumni

At Dnyanda English Medium School, our students shine in both scholastic and co-scholastic domains. From academic ranks to sports trophies, creative writing, and innovative robotics, our learners are constantly pushing their boundaries and setting new benchmarks of excellence.

Nurturing Talents: Student Journeys

Every child at Dnyanda has a unique path of discovery. Through activity-based learning and future-ready skill development programs like financial literacy and robotics, we foster critical thinking and help students manifest their full potential.

Scholastic Achievements

Our students consistently display strong learning milestones across CBSE core subjects:

  • Top ranks in Science & Cyber Olympiads.
  • Exceptional communication, vocabulary, and phonics skills.
  • Strong logical and analytical skills developed via financial literacy.
Co-Scholastic Milestones

We believe in a holistic learning experience that goes beyond the textbook:

  • Active participation and trophies in inter-school sports tournaments.
  • Creative exhibitions featuring paintings, handicrafts, and models.
  • Robotics projects demonstrating engineering and programming logic.
