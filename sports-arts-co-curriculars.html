Sports, Arts & Co-Curriculars

Developing core talents through active play, structured team sports, painting, crafts, and performing arts to foster physical and emotional resilience.

Encouraging Holistic Development

We believe that standard academic study should go hand-in-hand with active physical play and artistic expression. Our holistic curriculum ensures that child growth focuses on health, motor coordination, creative design capability, and self-confidence built through public speaking and stage performances.

Key Focus Areas

  • Structured outdoor sports
  • Active physical play and yoga
  • Painting, drawing, and crafts
  • Performing arts (drama and song)
  • Group team-building activities
  • Confidence & public speaking
  • Emotional resilience building
  • Creative writing & self-expression

Our Learning Approach

Education goes far beyond classrooms and textbooks. At Dnyanda English Medium School, our co-curricular program forms a core part of the weekly timetable. We encourage every student to actively participate in indoor and outdoor athletics, crafts projects, cultural performances, and social awareness clubs.

Why Choose Our Co-Curricular Programs?

  • Active Athletics: Introducing structured physical training (running, yoga, basic gymnastics) for motor health.
  • Arts & Design: Expert-led sessions in drawing, painting, paper-crafts, and clay modeling to refine hand-eye coordination.
  • Performing Arts: Annual events, musical classes, and school plays to boost confidence and team performance.
  • Teamwork Habits: Group activities that instil healthy competition, team communication, and leadership skills.
  • Creative Channeling: Providing children with a safe channel to explore their feelings, paint, write, and create.

Program Details

  • Age Group 3 - 10 Years
  • Section Level Co-Curricular Program
  • Integration Nursery to Grade V Curriculum
  • Availability Weekly Activities
