Admission Requirements

Education goes beyond textbooks and classrooms. We believe in empowering students to explore their passions challenge conventions.

Joining Dnyanda School

Admissions at Dnyanda English Medium School are simple, transparent, and structured to welcome young minds into a growth-oriented, value-based environment. We offer classes from Nursery and Preschool up to Grade V. Our admission criteria focus on basic readiness, social developmental age, and verification of foundational records. Parents are encouraged to visit our campus to experience our learning spaces first-hand.

Admissions Guidelines

1. Age Eligibility
Nursery / Preschool: Child must be 3 years of age by December 31st of the academic year.
Grade I: Child must be 6 years of age by December 31st of the academic year.
Grades II to V: Admission is based on progress report cards and corresponding age limits.
2. Required Documents
Original & Xerox Copy of Birth Certificate
Aadhaar Card of the Student & Parents
Original Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous recognized school (Grade II onwards)
Report Card of the last class attended
3 Passport-size photographs of the student
3. Admission Process
Form Inquiry: Obtain the admission form from the school office or schedule a visit.
Campus Tour & Interaction: Brief meeting of parents and the student with the coordinator.
Documentation & Fee Payment: Complete records verification and pay the registration/admission fees to secure enrollment.
4. Student Interaction
No stressful formal tests or exams are conducted for preschool and early grades.
A friendly verbal/interactive conversation with teachers is held to assess student's basic interests and concept readiness.
5. School Timings
Preschool Section (Nursery, Jr. KG, Sr. KG): Morning timings as scheduled by the administration.
Primary Section (Grade I to V): Full-day timings with standard scholastic periods, breaks, and sports activities.
